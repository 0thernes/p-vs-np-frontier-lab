# Statement-Fidelity Audit (`docs/statement-fidelity.md`)

Maps every formalized statement in `lean/Challenge.lean` and `lean/PalomarChallenge.lean` to its literature anchor (Defensible Independent AI Research Playbook v1.3, item #10). Verdicts follow the [2026-11-26 statement audit](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md): **Match** means the Lean statement is the stated claim; **Narrower** means it is a weaker or partial form of the literature result.

| ID | Lean Declaration (`Challenge.lean`) | Literature Anchor | Fidelity & Boundary Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `C1` | `PSubsetNPStmt` | Textbook $\mathrm{P}^O \subseteq \mathrm{NP}^O$ | Explicit 4-tape oracle Turing machine model in `PVsNP.OTM` with $(n+1)^c + c$ step bounds; equivalence with other models not formalized. | **Match** (this model) |
| `C2` | `BakerGillSolovayStmt` | Baker, Gill & Solovay, *SIAM J. Comput.* 4(4) (1975) | Explicitly defined collapse oracle $A$ (padded universal evaluator) and diagonalization oracle $B$. Oracles are arbitrary functions; computability is not part of the statement. | **Match** (this model) |
| `C3` | `NaturalProofBreaksPRFStmt` | Razborov & Rudich, *JCSS* 55(1) (1997) | Counting step only: exact integer bound for fixed $n, k$, numer/denom. The property is an arbitrary function and the PRF family has no hardness condition; constructivity, circuit class, pseudorandomness and asymptotics are absent. | **Narrower** |
| `C4` | `HybridTelescopingStmt` | Goldreich–Goldwasser–Micali (1986) hybrid argument | Pigeonhole form on integer sequences: $mB < \lvert a_0 - a_m\rvert \Rightarrow \exists i < m,\ \lvert a_i - a_{i+1}\rvert > B$. Not the summed triangle inequality stated in the paper. | **Narrower** (vs. paper) |
| `C5` | `NaturalProofNonvacuousStmt` | Non-vacuity witness for `IsLarge` / `IsUsefulAgainst` | `nonConstantProp` accepts $\ge 1/2$ of all tables and rejects constants for all $n \ge 1$. | **Match** |
| `C6` | `OddCycleContextualStmt` | Abramsky–Brandenburger (2011) contextuality | For all $k \ge 0$ the XOR system on $2k+1$ variables is minimally unsatisfiable. `IsMaximallyContextual` is minimal unsatisfiability; no presheaf or cohomology is formalized. | **Match** (as minimal unsatisfiability) |
| `C7` | `MerminPeresContextualStmt` | Peres (1990), Mermin (1990) $3\times 3$ square | The 6-equation, 9-variable Mermin–Peres parity system is minimally unsatisfiable. | **Match** (as minimal unsatisfiability) |
| `C8` | `TractablePolymorphismsStmt` | Schaefer (1978), Jeavons (1998) | Four preservation facts: $x \oplus y \oplus z$, AND, OR, majority on one XOR, Horn, dual-Horn and 2-clause relation each. Not Schaefer's dichotomy theorem. | **Match** (four facts) |
| `C9` | `Monotone3SATRigidStmt` | Post's lattice (clones $T_0$, $T_1$); Schaefer (1978) | For all finite arities, $\{R_{000}, R_{111}\}$ admits only projections and dropping either admits a constant. Classical fact, new Lean proof. NP-hardness consequences remain conditional on $\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$. | **Match** |
| `C10` | `MarkovInverterTightStmt` | Markov (1957/58) bounded-negation theorem | Lower bound ($n \ge 2^r \Rightarrow$ impossible with $\le r$ NOTs) and an explicit inverter with $k$ NOTs for exactly $n = 2^k - 1$ inputs. | **Narrower** ($n = 2^k - 1$ only) |
| `C11` | `FischerReductionStmt` | Fischer (1975) negation-limited compilation | Every circuit on exactly $2^k - 1$ inputs has an equivalent circuit with exactly $k$ NOT gates. No size bound; the inverter used is exponential-size. Fischer's theorem is the polynomial-size construction. | **Narrower** |
| `C12` | `MultiplicativeComplexityStmt` | Schnorr (1988), Boyar–Peralta (2008, *Theoret. Comput. Sci.* 396) | Every wire with $a$ AND gates has $\deg_{\mathbb{F}_2} \le a + 1$ over $\{\oplus, \land, 1\}$; hence $\mathrm{MC}(\mathrm{AND}_n) \ge n - 1$. | **Match** |
| `C13` | `PermDetChar2Stmt` | Valiant (1979) | $\mathrm{Perm}_n(A) \equiv \det_n(A) \pmod 2$ for all $A \in \mathbb{Z}^{n\times n}$ (Laplace definitions); $W_n$ has permanent 2 and determinant 0, so they differ mod every $p \ge 3$. No hardness notion. | **Match** |
| `C14` | `CatalyticAlgebraStmt` | Ben-Or & Cleve (1992); Buhrman et al. (2014) | Reversed inverted program undoes any well-formed straight-line register program; one recursive program of $3\cdot 4^{d-1} - 2$ instructions adds $\pm$ the product of $d$ program constants and restores all other registers. No space or time model. | **Narrower** |

Anchors Goldreich–Goldwasser–Micali 1986, Jeavons 1998, Post's lattice, Schnorr 1988 and Ben-Or–Cleve 1992 are not bibitems in `paper/main.tex`; see `ERRATA.md`.
