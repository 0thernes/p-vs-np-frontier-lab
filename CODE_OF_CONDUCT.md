# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, being respectful of differing technical opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on reproducible mathematical and scientific evidence.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, or fabricating scientific claims.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to Alexander S. Donahue at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
